Answers about ReplyInPublic

ReplyInPublic is a web inbox for comments customers have already posted in public. A person decides what to answer. The product does not post the reply.

By Pranav Kaja. Last updated 16 November 2026.

What is ReplyInPublic?

An inbox that groups public customer comments about the same issue, for five kitchen brands, behind a sign in.

Public comments vs surveys

A survey starts when the brand asks. A public comment starts when the customer writes, with no prompt.

How to answer a public comment

Sign in, read the inbox, open the issue, then park a reply, follow up, or keep the feedback. You send the reply.

FAQ

Posting, sources, the five brands, sign in, and price.
