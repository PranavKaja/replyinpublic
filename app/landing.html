The core thesis behind ReplyInPublic

The shift from surveys to public replies

Why modern consumer brands are reallocating feedback budgets into public resolution.

Legacy model

Private feedback surveys

  • Prompted bias: Only captured when an email or pop-up interrupts the customer.
  • High cost: Expensive survey platforms, agency retainers, and cash incentives.
  • Locked data: Sits inside internal slide decks while online complaints stay unanswered.
  • Zero public return: Prospective customers looking up reviews never see the effort.
ReplyInPublic

Public comment resolution

  • Raw honesty: Unprompted feedback posted where real buying conversations happen.
  • Zero collection cost: Free customer signal already existing on public surfaces.
  • Grounded in manuals: Suggested answers cite verified warranty and FAQ pages.
  • Compounding reputation: Every thread resolved publicly earns trust with thousands of lurkers.

How ReplyInPublic works

01 / Detect

Listen to the room

We watch YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X, and Reddit for unprompted questions, complaints, and purchase intent. Noise stays out of the queue.

02 / Ground

Draft strictly from manuals

Replies are generated using your public warranty and FAQ documents. If the manuals don't have the answer, the copilot routes to your support contact.

03 / Earn

Approve with one click

A person on your team approves or edits every draft. Once approved, it enters the queue to be published directly to the public thread.
