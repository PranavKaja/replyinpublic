Ninja 5-Year Limited Warranty. The five year limited warranty applies to purchases made from authorized retailers of SharkNinja Operating LLC. Warranty coverage applies to the original owner and to the original product only and is not transferable. SharkNinja warrants that the unit shall be free from defects in material and workmanship for a period of five years from the date of purchase when it is used under normal household conditions and maintained according to the Owner's Guide. In the event a replacement unit is issued, the warranty coverage ends six months following the receipt date of the replacement unit or the remainder of the existing warranty, whichever is later. If the appliance fails to operate properly while in use under normal household conditions within the warranty period, visit ninjakitchen.com/support for product care and maintenance self-help. Customer Service Specialists are available at 1-866-826-6941. Register the product at registeryourninja.com and have the product on hand when you call. You must call 1-866-826-6941 to initiate a warranty claim and you will need the receipt as proof of purchase.
